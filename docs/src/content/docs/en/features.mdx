## Switcher

<LightEntry no="D1.1" name="List view" keys={["⌘", "Tab"]}>
  Lists app icons and window titles. It takes no thumbnails, so it opens at once
  and uses almost no memory.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D1.2" name="Window-level switching" keys={["⇧", "⌘", "Tab"]}>
  Lists one window per row in most-recently-used order. Other windows of the
  same app, and Chrome or Vivaldi PWAs, get their own rows.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D1.3" name="Shortcut hints" keys={["fi"]}>
  Shows the filter characters at the left of each row.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry
  no="D1.4"
  name="Grouped minimized and hidden rows"
  setting="Settings › Filter"
>
  Keeps minimized windows and hidden apps below the list. They can also be mixed
  in or hidden.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D1.5" name="Fullscreen handling" setting="Settings › Filter">
  Fullscreen windows on their own Space can also be mixed in, hidden, or kept
  below.
</LightEntry>

## Search

<LightEntry no="D2.1" name="Type to filter" keys={["A–Z"]}>
  Typing in the switcher narrows the list.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D2.2" name="Open ready to filter" keys={["⌘", "Space"]}>
  Opens the switcher already waiting for input.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D2.3" name="Romaji search" setting="Settings › Filter">
  Matches kana and kanji readings from romaji.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D2.4" name="Fuzzy search" setting="Settings › Filter">
  Matches when the characters appear in order, even when they are not next to
  each other.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D2.5" name="Sort order" setting="Settings › Filter">
  Sorts by most recently used, or by best match.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D2.6" name="Shortcut memory" setting="Settings › Filter">
  Remembers the chosen window per typed characters. It remembers 5 characters by
  default, and 0 turns it off.
</LightEntry>

## Appearance

<LightEntry no="D3.1" name="Appearance" setting="Settings › Appearance">
  Follows the system, or stays on light or dark.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D3.2" name="Text size" setting="Settings › Appearance">
  Changes the text size of the switcher.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D3.3" name="Liquid Glass">
  Matches the Liquid Glass look of macOS Tahoe.
</LightEntry>

## Keyboard

<LightEntry no="D4.1" name="Keybindings" setting="Settings › Keyboard">
  Changes the keys for show, reverse, filter, next, previous, commit, and
  cancel. More than one key can be assigned to one action.
</LightEntry>

## General

<LightEntry no="D5.1" name="Launch at login" setting="Settings › General">
  Starts automatically when you log in to the Mac.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D5.2" name="Check for updates" setting="Settings › General">
  Checks for new releases. Stable follows releases only, Beta includes
  prereleases.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D5.3" name="Minimal permissions">
  Needs only Accessibility and Input Monitoring. No Screen Recording means no
  weekly re-confirmation dialog.
</LightEntry>

<LightEntry no="D5.4" name="Menu bar app">
  Lives in the menu bar with no Dock icon.
</LightEntry>

See [Usage](./usage/) for the full steps.
